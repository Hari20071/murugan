Chickenpox

Chickenpox is a highly contagious viral infection caused by the Varicella-Zoster virus. It commonly affects children but can occur at any age. It causes itchy skin rashes with red spots and fluid-filled blisters.

Symptoms of Chickenpox

  • Fever
  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Loss of appetite
  • Headache
  • Itchy red rash
  • Fluid-filled blisters
  • Body pain

How to Cure / Treatment

Chickenpox usually heals on its own within 7–14 days. Treatment mainly focuses on relieving symptoms and preventing complications.

  • Take adequate rest
  • Drink plenty of fluids
  • Keep skin clean and avoid scratching
  • Use calamine lotion for itching
  • Take lukewarm baths

Tablets / Medicines Used

  • Paracetamol (for fever)
  • Antihistamines (for itching)
  • Acyclovir (antiviral medicine – prescribed by doctor)

Note: Always consult a doctor before taking any medication.

Prevention

  • Varicella vaccine
  • Avoid close contact with infected person
  • Maintain good hygiene
