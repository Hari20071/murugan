Complete Information About Allergy

Allergy

An allergy is an overreaction of the immune system to substances that are usually harmless. These substances are called allergens and may include pollen, dust, certain foods, medicines, or animal dander.

Types of Allergies

  • Food Allergy
  • Skin Allergy
  • Dust Allergy
  • Pollen Allergy (Hay Fever)
  • Drug Allergy
  • Insect Sting Allergy

Symptoms of Allergy

  • Sneezing and runny nose
  • Itchy or watery eyes
  • Skin rash or hives
  • Swelling of lips, face, or throat
  • Coughing or wheezing
  • Shortness of breath (in severe cases)

Common Causes

  • Pollen from plants
  • Dust mites
  • Animal dander
  • Certain foods (nuts, milk, eggs)
  • Medicines
  • Insect stings

How to Cure / Treat Allergy?

✔ Avoid known allergens.
✔ Keep surroundings clean and dust-free.
✔ Use air purifiers if needed.
✔ Follow doctor’s prescribed treatment.
✔ Emergency care for severe allergic reactions.

Tablets Used for Allergy

Doctors may prescribe:

  • Cetirizine
  • Loratadine
  • Fexofenadine
  • Diphenhydramine
  • Montelukast

⚠ In severe cases (anaphylaxis), immediate medical attention is required. Always take medication under medical supervision.

Seek emergency help if symptoms include difficulty breathing, severe swelling, or loss of consciousness.

Prevention Tips

  • Identify and avoid triggers
  • Maintain personal hygiene
  • Keep home clean and ventilated
  • Carry prescribed emergency medication if required
