What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a value used to measure whether a person has a healthy body weight based on their height and weight. It helps to classify individuals as underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese.

How to Calculate BMI?

BMI is calculated by dividing a person’s weight in kilograms by the square of their height in meters.

Formula:
BMI = Weight (kg) / Height (m)2

Example:

If a person weighs 70 kg and their height is 1.75 meters:

BMI = 70 / (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.86

BMI Categories:

  • Below 18.5 – Underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9 – Normal weight
  • 25 – 29.9 – Overweight
  • 30 and above – Obese
