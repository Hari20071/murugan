Complete Information About Hypertension

Hypertension

Hypertension, also known as High Blood Pressure, is a condition in which the force of blood against the artery walls is consistently too high. Normal blood pressure is around 120/80 mmHg. If it remains above 140/90 mmHg, it is considered hypertension.

Common Symptoms

Hypertension is often called a "silent killer" because many people do not notice symptoms. However, some may experience:

  • Headache
  • Dizziness
  • Shortness of breath
  • Chest pain
  • Nosebleeds
  • Blurred vision

Causes

  • High salt intake
  • Obesity
  • Stress
  • Lack of physical activity
  • Smoking and alcohol
  • Family history

Risk Factors

  • Age above 40 years
  • Diabetes
  • Kidney disease
  • High cholesterol

How to Manage / Treat Hypertension?

✔ Reduce salt intake
✔ Exercise regularly
✔ Maintain healthy weight
✔ Avoid smoking and alcohol
✔ Manage stress
✔ Take prescribed medicines regularly

Tablets Used for Hypertension

Common medicines prescribed by doctors include:

  • Amlodipine
  • Losartan
  • Enalapril
  • Atenolol
  • Hydrochlorothiazide

⚠ Always consult a doctor before taking any blood pressure medication. Do not stop medicines without medical advice.

Prevention Tips

  • Check blood pressure regularly
  • Eat more fruits and vegetables
  • Limit processed foods
  • Sleep 7–8 hours daily
  • Stay physically active
