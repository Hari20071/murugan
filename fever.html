Complete Information About Fever

Fever

Fever is a temporary increase in body temperature, usually caused by an infection. The normal body temperature is around 98.6°F (37°C). Fever is generally considered when the body temperature rises above 100.4°F (38°C).

Common Symptoms of Fever

  • High body temperature
  • Headache
  • Muscle pain
  • Weakness and fatigue
  • Sweating
  • Chills and shivering
  • Loss of appetite

Causes of Fever

  • Viral infections (cold, flu)
  • Bacterial infections
  • Dengue or malaria
  • Inflammation
  • Heat exhaustion

How to Cure Fever?

✔ Take proper rest
✔ Drink plenty of fluids (water, juice, ORS)
✔ Keep body cool
✔ Eat light and healthy food

Tablets Used for Fever

The most commonly used tablets for reducing fever are:

  • Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)
  • Ibuprofen
  • Aspirin (for adults only)

⚠ Always consult a doctor before taking any medication, especially for children, pregnant women, or if fever lasts more than 3 days.

Prevention Tips

  • Wash hands regularly
  • Maintain personal hygiene
  • Drink clean water
  • Get proper vaccination
