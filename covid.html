Complete Information About COVID-19

COVID-19

COVID-19 is a contagious respiratory disease caused by the coronavirus SARS-CoV-2. It primarily affects the lungs but can also impact other organs. The disease spread globally during the 2019–2020 pandemic.

Common Symptoms

  • Fever
  • Dry cough
  • Shortness of breath
  • Loss of taste or smell
  • Fatigue
  • Body aches
  • Sore throat
  • Headache

Causes

  • Infection by SARS-CoV-2 virus
  • Close contact with infected person
  • Exposure to respiratory droplets

How COVID-19 Spreads

  • Through cough or sneeze droplets
  • Airborne transmission in closed spaces
  • Touching contaminated surfaces
  • Close contact without mask protection

How to Treat / Manage COVID-19?

✔ Isolate at home (mild cases)
✔ Take proper rest
✔ Drink plenty of fluids
✔ Monitor oxygen level
✔ Seek hospital care if symptoms worsen

Medicines Used for COVID-19

Medicines are prescribed based on severity and doctor evaluation:

  • Paracetamol (for fever)
  • Antiviral medicines (e.g., Remdesivir)
  • Molnupiravir (in some cases)
  • Corticosteroids (e.g., Dexamethasone)
  • Oxygen therapy (for severe cases)

⚠ Do not self-medicate. Always consult a healthcare professional for proper treatment.

Prevention Tips

  • Get vaccinated
  • Wear mask in crowded places
  • Wash hands frequently
  • Maintain social distancing
  • Avoid crowded indoor spaces
