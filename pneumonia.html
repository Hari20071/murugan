Complete Information About Pneumonia

Pneumonia

Pneumonia is a lung infection that causes inflammation in the air sacs (alveoli) of one or both lungs. The air sacs may fill with fluid or pus, leading to cough, fever, chills, and difficulty breathing.

Symptoms of Pneumonia

  • Chest pain while breathing or coughing
  • Persistent cough (with or without mucus)
  • High fever and chills
  • Shortness of breath
  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Nausea or vomiting

Causes of Pneumonia

  • Bacterial infection
  • Viral infection
  • Fungal infection
  • Inhalation of harmful substances

Risk Factors

  • Children under 5 years
  • Adults over 65 years
  • Smoking
  • Weak immune system
  • Chronic diseases (diabetes, heart disease)

How to Cure / Treat Pneumonia?

✔ Take prescribed antibiotics (for bacterial pneumonia).
✔ Get plenty of rest.
✔ Drink fluids to stay hydrated.
✔ Use fever reducers if recommended by doctor.
✔ Hospital care may be needed in severe cases.

Tablets Used for Pneumonia

Doctors may prescribe:

  • Azithromycin
  • Amoxicillin
  • Doxycycline
  • Paracetamol (for fever)

⚠ Always consult a healthcare professional before taking any medication. Self-medication can be harmful.

Prevention Tips

  • Get vaccinated
  • Wash hands regularly
  • Avoid smoking
  • Maintain strong immunity with healthy diet
  • Avoid close contact with infected individuals
