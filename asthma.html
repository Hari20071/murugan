Complete Information About Asthma

Asthma

Asthma is a chronic respiratory disease that affects the airways of the lungs. It causes the airways to become inflamed, narrow, and produce extra mucus, making it difficult to breathe.

Common Symptoms

  • Shortness of breath
  • Wheezing (whistling sound while breathing)
  • Chest tightness
  • Coughing (especially at night or early morning)
  • Difficulty breathing during exercise

Causes

  • Genetic factors
  • Allergies
  • Respiratory infections
  • Environmental pollution
  • Exposure to smoke

Common Triggers

  • Dust and pollen
  • Cold air
  • Pet dander
  • Strong perfumes or chemicals
  • Exercise (in some cases)

How to Manage / Treat Asthma?

✔ Avoid known triggers
✔ Use inhaler as prescribed
✔ Take regular medications
✔ Maintain clean environment
✔ Regular medical checkups

Medicines Used for Asthma

Common medicines prescribed by doctors include:

  • Salbutamol Inhaler (Quick relief)
  • Budesonide Inhaler
  • Montelukast tablets
  • Theophylline
  • Prednisolone (for severe cases)

⚠ Always consult a doctor before taking asthma medication. Do not stop inhalers suddenly without medical advice.

Prevention Tips

  • Keep home dust-free
  • Avoid smoking
  • Use mask in polluted areas
  • Get flu vaccination
  • Follow asthma action plan
