Complete Information About Thyroid Disorder

Thyroid Disorder

Thyroid disorder occurs when the thyroid gland (a butterfly-shaped gland in the neck) produces too much or too little thyroid hormone. These hormones control metabolism, heart rate, body temperature, and energy levels.

Types of Thyroid Disorders

  • Hypothyroidism – Low thyroid hormone production
  • Hyperthyroidism – Excess thyroid hormone production

Symptoms

Hypothyroidism Symptoms:

  • Weight gain
  • Fatigue
  • Cold intolerance
  • Dry skin
  • Hair loss
  • Slow heart rate

Hyperthyroidism Symptoms:

  • Weight loss
  • Rapid heartbeat
  • Excessive sweating
  • Anxiety or irritability
  • Tremors
  • Heat intolerance

Common Causes

  • Autoimmune diseases (Graves’ disease, Hashimoto’s thyroiditis)
  • Iodine imbalance
  • Thyroid surgery
  • Genetic factors

How to Cure / Treat Thyroid Disorder?

✔ Regular thyroid hormone testing (TSH test).
✔ Daily medication as prescribed.
✔ Balanced iodine intake.
✔ Healthy diet and regular exercise.
✔ Surgery or radioactive therapy in severe cases.

Tablets Used for Thyroid Disorder

Doctors may prescribe:

  • Levothyroxine (for hypothyroidism)
  • Methimazole (for hyperthyroidism)
  • Propylthiouracil (PTU)
  • Beta-blockers (to control symptoms)

⚠ Medication should only be taken under medical supervision. Dosage depends on blood test results.

Prevention Tips

  • Use iodized salt
  • Regular health check-ups
  • Maintain healthy lifestyle
  • Avoid self-medication
