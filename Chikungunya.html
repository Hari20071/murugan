Complete Information About Chikungunya

Chikungunya

Chikungunya is a viral disease transmitted to humans through the bite of infected Aedes mosquitoes. It causes sudden fever and severe joint pain. The disease is common in tropical regions.

Symptoms of Chikungunya

  • High fever
  • Severe joint pain
  • Muscle pain
  • Headache
  • Skin rash
  • Fatigue
  • Swelling in joints

How Does It Spread?

  • Through bite of infected Aedes mosquitoes
  • Common during rainy seasons
  • Not spread directly from person to person

How to Cure / Treat Chikungunya?

✔ There is no specific antiviral treatment.
✔ Take adequate rest.
✔ Drink plenty of fluids.
✔ Use prescribed medicines to reduce fever and pain.
✔ Hospital care in severe cases.

Tablets Used for Chikungunya

Doctors may recommend:

  • Paracetamol (for fever and pain)

⚠ Avoid Aspirin and Ibuprofen unless prescribed, as they may increase bleeding risk. Always consult a doctor.

Seek medical attention if symptoms worsen or if there is persistent joint pain.

Prevention Tips

  • Use mosquito repellents
  • Wear full-sleeved clothes
  • Remove stagnant water around home
  • Use mosquito nets
  • Keep surroundings clean
