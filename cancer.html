Complete Information About Cancer

Cancer

Cancer is a disease in which abnormal cells grow uncontrollably and may spread to other parts of the body. It can affect almost any organ. Early detection and proper treatment increase the chances of recovery.

Types of Cancer

  • Lung Cancer
  • Breast Cancer
  • Blood Cancer (Leukemia)
  • Skin Cancer
  • Prostate Cancer
  • Colon Cancer

Common Symptoms

  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Persistent fatigue
  • Pain in specific areas
  • Lumps or swelling
  • Changes in skin appearance
  • Persistent cough
  • Abnormal bleeding

Causes

  • Genetic mutations
  • Smoking and tobacco use
  • Radiation exposure
  • Unhealthy lifestyle
  • Environmental pollution
  • Family history

How to Treat / Manage Cancer?

✔ Early diagnosis through screening tests
✔ Surgery (removal of tumor)
✔ Chemotherapy
✔ Radiation therapy
✔ Immunotherapy
✔ Targeted therapy

Medicines Used for Cancer

Medicines depend on the type and stage of cancer. Common treatments include:

  • Chemotherapy drugs (e.g., Cyclophosphamide)
  • Cisplatin
  • Doxorubicin
  • Immunotherapy drugs (e.g., Nivolumab)
  • Targeted therapy drugs

⚠ Cancer treatment must be done under specialist supervision. Self-medication is strictly dangerous.

Prevention Tips

  • Avoid tobacco and smoking
  • Eat healthy balanced diet
  • Exercise regularly
  • Protect skin from excessive sunlight
  • Get regular medical checkups
  • Take recommended vaccinations
