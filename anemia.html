Complete Information About Anemia

Anemia

Anemia is a condition in which the body does not have enough healthy red blood cells to carry adequate oxygen to tissues. This results in fatigue, weakness, and other health problems.

Symptoms of Anemia

  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Pale or yellowish skin
  • Shortness of breath
  • Dizziness or lightheadedness
  • Cold hands and feet
  • Irregular heartbeat
  • Headache

Common Causes

  • Iron deficiency
  • Vitamin B12 deficiency
  • Folate deficiency
  • Chronic diseases
  • Blood loss
  • Genetic disorders

Types of Anemia

  • Iron-Deficiency Anemia
  • Vitamin Deficiency Anemia
  • Aplastic Anemia
  • Sickle Cell Anemia

How to Cure / Treat Anemia?

✔ Increase iron-rich foods (spinach, beans, red meat).
✔ Take prescribed iron or vitamin supplements.
✔ Treat underlying medical conditions.
✔ Blood transfusion in severe cases.
✔ Regular blood tests to monitor levels.

Tablets Used for Anemia

Doctors may prescribe:

  • Iron tablets (Ferrous sulfate)
  • Vitamin B12 supplements
  • Folic acid tablets
  • Multivitamin supplements

⚠ Always consult a healthcare professional before taking supplements. Self-medication may cause complications.

Prevention Tips

  • Eat balanced diet rich in iron and vitamins
  • Regular health check-ups
  • Take supplements only if prescribed
  • Maintain healthy lifestyle
