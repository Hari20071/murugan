Measles

Measles is a highly contagious viral infection caused by the measles virus. It spreads through coughing and sneezing and mainly affects children, but adults can also be infected.

Symptoms of Measles

  • High fever
  • Runny nose
  • Dry cough
  • Red, watery eyes
  • Sore throat
  • Small white spots inside the mouth (Koplik spots)
  • Red skin rash spreading from face to body

How to Cure / Treatment

There is no specific antiviral cure for measles. Treatment focuses on relieving symptoms and preventing complications.

  • Take complete bed rest
  • Drink plenty of fluids
  • Maintain good nutrition
  • Use humidifier for cough relief
  • Monitor for complications

Tablets / Medicines Used

  • Paracetamol (for fever)
  • Vitamin A supplements
  • Cough syrups (as prescribed)
  • Antibiotics (if secondary bacterial infection occurs)

Note: Medicines should only be taken under medical supervision.

Prevention

  • MMR Vaccine (Measles, Mumps, Rubella)
  • Avoid close contact with infected individuals
  • Maintain proper hygiene
  • Boost immunity with healthy diet
