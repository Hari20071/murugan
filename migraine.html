Complete Information About Migraine

Migraine

Migraine is a neurological condition that causes severe, recurring headaches. It is often accompanied by nausea, sensitivity to light, and visual disturbances. Migraine attacks can last from a few hours to several days.

Common Symptoms

  • Severe throbbing headache (usually one side)
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Sensitivity to light (photophobia)
  • Sensitivity to sound
  • Blurred vision
  • Aura (flashes of light or zigzag lines)

Causes

  • Genetic factors
  • Hormonal changes
  • Stress
  • Lack of sleep
  • Certain foods and drinks

Common Triggers

  • Bright lights
  • Loud noises
  • Skipping meals
  • Dehydration
  • Strong smells
  • Weather changes

How to Treat / Manage Migraine?

✔ Rest in a quiet, dark room
✔ Drink plenty of water
✔ Apply cold compress on forehead
✔ Manage stress effectively
✔ Follow regular sleep schedule

Tablets Used for Migraine

Common medicines prescribed by doctors include:

  • Paracetamol
  • Ibuprofen
  • Sumatriptan
  • Rizatriptan
  • Propranolol (preventive)

⚠ Consult a doctor before taking migraine medications, especially for frequent or severe attacks.

Prevention Tips

  • Avoid known triggers
  • Maintain proper sleep routine
  • Eat regular meals
  • Exercise regularly
  • Stay hydrated
