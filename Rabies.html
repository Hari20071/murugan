Rabies

Rabies is a deadly viral disease that spreads through the bite or scratch of an infected animal. It affects the central nervous system and can lead to brain damage and death if not treated immediately. Dogs are the most common source of human rabies transmission.

Symptoms of Rabies

  • Fever
  • Headache
  • Weakness or fatigue
  • Pain or tingling at bite site
  • Anxiety and confusion
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Fear of water (Hydrophobia)
  • Excessive salivation

How to Cure / Treatment

Rabies has no cure once symptoms appear. Immediate treatment after an animal bite can prevent the disease.

  • Wash the wound immediately with soap and water for 15 minutes
  • Apply antiseptic
  • Take Rabies vaccine as soon as possible
  • Rabies Immunoglobulin (RIG) injection if required

Tablets / Medicines Used

  • Rabies Vaccine (series of injections)
  • Rabies Immunoglobulin (RIG)
  • Pain relief medicines (as prescribed)
  • Antibiotics (if wound infection occurs)

Important: Rabies prevention treatment must be taken under medical supervision immediately after exposure.

Prevention

  • Vaccinate pets regularly
  • Avoid contact with stray animals
  • Seek medical care immediately after animal bites
  • Community vaccination programs
