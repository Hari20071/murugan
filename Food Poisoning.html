Food Poisoning

Food poisoning is an illness caused by consuming contaminated food or drinks. It may be caused by bacteria, viruses, parasites, or toxins present in spoiled or improperly cooked food.

Symptoms of Food Poisoning

  • Nausea
  • Vomiting
  • Diarrhea
  • Stomach cramps
  • Fever
  • Weakness
  • Dehydration

How to Cure / Treatment

Most cases of food poisoning improve within a few days. Treatment mainly focuses on replacing lost fluids and relieving symptoms.

  • Drink plenty of water
  • Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS)
  • Take adequate rest
  • Eat light and soft foods
  • Avoid oily and spicy food

Tablets / Medicines Used

  • ORS packets (for dehydration)
  • Paracetamol (for fever)
  • Antiemetic medicines (for vomiting)
  • Antibiotics (only if prescribed by doctor)
  • Probiotics (to restore gut bacteria)

Note: Do not take medicines without consulting a doctor, especially in severe cases.

Prevention

  • Wash hands before eating
  • Cook food properly
  • Avoid stale or spoiled food
  • Store food at correct temperature
  • Drink clean and safe water
