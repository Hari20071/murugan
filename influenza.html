Complete Information About Influenza (Flu)

Influenza

Influenza, commonly known as the flu, is a contagious viral infection that affects the respiratory system (nose, throat, and lungs). It spreads easily from person to person, especially during seasonal outbreaks.

Common Symptoms

  • High fever
  • Cough
  • Sore throat
  • Runny or blocked nose
  • Body aches
  • Headache
  • Fatigue
  • Chills and sweating

Causes

  • Influenza virus type A
  • Influenza virus type B
  • Influenza virus type C (mild cases)

How Influenza Spreads

  • Through cough or sneeze droplets
  • Close contact with infected person
  • Touching contaminated surfaces

How to Cure / Manage Influenza?

✔ Take proper rest
✔ Drink plenty of fluids
✔ Eat nutritious food
✔ Use fever reducers if needed
✔ Isolate to prevent spreading

Tablets Used for Influenza

Common medicines prescribed by doctors include:

  • Paracetamol (for fever and pain)
  • Ibuprofen
  • Oseltamivir (Tamiflu) – Antiviral medicine
  • Zanamivir

⚠ Always consult a doctor before taking antiviral medications. Antibiotics are not effective against viral infections unless there is a bacterial complication.

Prevention Tips

  • Get annual flu vaccination
  • Wash hands frequently
  • Cover mouth while coughing
  • Avoid close contact with infected individuals
  • Maintain strong immunity
