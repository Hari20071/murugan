Complete Information About Kidney Disease

Kidney Disease

Kidney disease occurs when the kidneys lose their ability to filter waste and excess fluids from the blood effectively. It can develop gradually (chronic kidney disease) or suddenly (acute kidney injury).

Symptoms of Kidney Disease

  • Swelling in legs, ankles, or feet
  • Frequent urination (especially at night)
  • Blood in urine
  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Loss of appetite
  • High blood pressure
  • Shortness of breath

Common Causes

  • Diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • Kidney infections
  • Genetic disorders
  • Long-term use of certain medicines

Types of Kidney Disease

  • Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)
  • Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)
  • Kidney Stones
  • Polycystic Kidney Disease

How to Cure / Treat Kidney Disease?

✔ Control blood sugar and blood pressure.
✔ Follow a kidney-friendly diet.
✔ Reduce salt intake.
✔ Avoid smoking and alcohol.
✔ Dialysis may be required in advanced stages.
✔ Kidney transplant in severe cases.

Tablets Used for Kidney Disease

Doctors may prescribe medicines such as:

  • ACE inhibitors (for blood pressure control)
  • Angiotensin II receptor blockers (ARBs)
  • Diuretics
  • Iron supplements
  • Phosphate binders

⚠ Always consult a healthcare professional before taking any medication. Treatment depends on the stage and cause of the disease.

Prevention Tips

  • Maintain healthy blood pressure
  • Control diabetes
  • Stay hydrated
  • Exercise regularly
  • Avoid overuse of painkillers
