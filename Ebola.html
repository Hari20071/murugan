Ebola

Ebola is a severe and often fatal viral disease caused by the Ebola virus. It spreads through direct contact with infected body fluids such as blood, saliva, sweat, vomit, and other bodily secretions.

Symptoms of Ebola

  • Sudden high fever
  • Severe headache
  • Muscle and joint pain
  • Weakness and fatigue
  • Sore throat
  • Vomiting and diarrhea
  • Stomach pain
  • Unexplained bleeding or bruising (in severe cases)

How to Cure / Treatment

There is no simple home cure for Ebola. Immediate hospital care is required. Treatment focuses on supportive medical care.

  • IV fluids to prevent dehydration
  • Oxygen therapy
  • Maintaining blood pressure
  • Treating infections
  • Close medical monitoring

Medicines / Tablets Used

  • Supportive medications for fever and pain (Paracetamol)
  • Antiviral treatments (given in hospital settings)
  • Monoclonal antibody treatments (as prescribed by specialists)

Important: Ebola treatment must only be handled by trained medical professionals in hospitals.

Prevention

  • Avoid contact with infected persons
  • Wear protective equipment in affected areas
  • Practice proper hand hygiene
  • Safe burial practices
  • Vaccination in outbreak regions
