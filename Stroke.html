Stroke

Stroke is a serious medical condition that occurs when blood supply to a part of the brain is interrupted or reduced. Without oxygen and nutrients, brain cells begin to die within minutes. Stroke requires immediate medical attention.

Symptoms of Stroke

  • Sudden numbness or weakness in face, arm, or leg (especially one side)
  • Difficulty speaking or understanding speech
  • Sudden confusion
  • Severe headache
  • Loss of balance or coordination
  • Blurred or double vision

Emergency Sign: Use the FAST method – Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call emergency.

How to Cure / Treatment

Stroke is a medical emergency. Immediate hospital treatment is required. Treatment depends on the type of stroke (ischemic or hemorrhagic).

  • Clot-dissolving medications (for ischemic stroke)
  • Surgery in severe cases
  • Blood pressure control
  • Rehabilitation therapy (speech, physical therapy)

Tablets / Medicines Used

  • Aspirin (blood thinner)
  • Clopidogrel
  • Anticoagulants (as prescribed by doctor)
  • Blood pressure medicines
  • Cholesterol-lowering medicines (Statins)

Note: Medicines must only be taken under medical supervision.

Prevention

  • Control blood pressure
  • Maintain healthy diet
  • Regular exercise
  • Avoid smoking and alcohol
  • Control diabetes and cholesterol
