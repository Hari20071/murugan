Complete Information About Tuberculosis (TB)

Tuberculosis

Tuberculosis (TB) is a serious infectious disease caused by the bacterium Mycobacterium tuberculosis. It mainly affects the lungs (pulmonary TB) but can also affect other parts of the body such as the brain, kidneys, and spine.

Common Symptoms

  • Persistent cough lasting more than 2–3 weeks
  • Chest pain
  • Coughing up blood
  • Fever
  • Night sweats
  • Weight loss
  • Fatigue and weakness

Causes

  • Infection by Mycobacterium tuberculosis bacteria
  • Weak immune system
  • Close contact with infected person
  • Malnutrition

How Tuberculosis Spreads

  • Through air when an infected person coughs or sneezes
  • Close and prolonged contact with TB patient
  • Poor ventilation in crowded areas

How to Cure / Treat Tuberculosis?

✔ Early diagnosis through sputum test or chest X-ray
✔ Complete full course of TB medicines (6–9 months)
✔ Regular doctor follow-up
✔ Proper nutrition and rest
✔ Avoid spreading infection to others

Tablets Used for Tuberculosis

Standard TB treatment usually includes a combination of medicines such as:

  • Isoniazid
  • Rifampicin
  • Pyrazinamide
  • Ethambutol
  • Streptomycin (in some cases)

⚠ TB treatment must be completed fully as prescribed by a doctor. Stopping medicines early can cause drug-resistant TB.

Prevention Tips

  • BCG vaccination
  • Cover mouth while coughing
  • Improve ventilation in living areas
  • Early screening and treatment
  • Maintain strong immunity through healthy diet
