Complete Information About Arthritis

Arthritis

Arthritis is a condition that causes inflammation, pain, and stiffness in the joints. It can affect one or multiple joints and may worsen with age.

Types of Arthritis

  • Osteoarthritis
  • Rheumatoid Arthritis
  • Gout
  • Psoriatic Arthritis

Common Symptoms

  • Joint pain
  • Swelling
  • Stiffness
  • Reduced range of motion
  • Redness around joints

Causes

  • Age-related wear and tear
  • Autoimmune disorders
  • Joint injuries
  • Obesity
  • Genetic factors

How to Treat / Manage Arthritis?

✔ Regular exercise and physiotherapy
✔ Maintain healthy body weight
✔ Hot and cold therapy
✔ Balanced diet
✔ Regular medical consultation

Tablets Used for Arthritis

Common medicines prescribed by doctors include:

  • Ibuprofen
  • Diclofenac
  • Paracetamol
  • Methotrexate (for rheumatoid arthritis)
  • Colchicine (for gout)

⚠ Always consult a doctor before taking arthritis medication. Long-term use of painkillers should be monitored by a healthcare professional.

Prevention Tips

  • Maintain ideal body weight
  • Stay physically active
  • Avoid joint injuries
  • Eat anti-inflammatory foods
  • Regular health checkups
