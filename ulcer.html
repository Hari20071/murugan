Complete Information About Ulcer

Ulcer

An ulcer is an open sore that develops on the lining of the stomach, small intestine, or esophagus. The most common type is a peptic ulcer, which includes gastric ulcers (stomach) and duodenal ulcers (small intestine).

Types of Ulcers

  • Gastric Ulcer – Occurs in the stomach
  • Duodenal Ulcer – Occurs in the small intestine
  • Esophageal Ulcer – Occurs in the esophagus
  • Mouth Ulcer – Occurs inside the mouth

Symptoms of Ulcer

  • Burning stomach pain
  • Bloating
  • Heartburn
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Loss of appetite
  • Weight loss
  • Dark or bloody stools (severe cases)

Common Causes

  • Infection with Helicobacter pylori (H. pylori)
  • Long-term use of painkillers (NSAIDs)
  • Excess stomach acid
  • Smoking
  • Stress (can worsen symptoms)

How to Cure / Treat Ulcer?

✔ Take prescribed medications regularly.
✔ Avoid spicy and acidic foods.
✔ Stop smoking and limit alcohol.
✔ Reduce stress.
✔ Follow doctor’s dietary advice.

Tablets Used for Ulcer

Doctors may prescribe:

  • Omeprazole
  • Pantoprazole
  • Ranitidine
  • Amoxicillin (if H. pylori infection)
  • Clarithromycin

⚠ Always take medications under medical supervision. Complete the full course if antibiotics are prescribed.

Seek immediate medical attention if you experience severe abdominal pain, vomiting blood, or black stools.

Prevention Tips

  • Eat balanced meals regularly
  • Avoid excessive painkiller use
  • Practice good hygiene to prevent infection
  • Manage stress effectively
  • Avoid smoking and alcohol
