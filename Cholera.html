Complete Information About Cholera

Cholera

Cholera is a serious bacterial infection caused by Vibrio cholerae. It spreads through contaminated food and water and mainly affects the intestines, causing severe diarrhea.

Symptoms of Cholera

  • Severe watery diarrhea
  • Vomiting
  • Dehydration
  • Muscle cramps
  • Rapid heart rate
  • Low blood pressure
  • Extreme thirst

How Does It Spread?

  • Contaminated drinking water
  • Improperly cooked food
  • Poor sanitation and hygiene
  • Flood-affected areas

How to Cure / Treat Cholera?

✔ Immediate rehydration (ORS solution).
✔ Drink plenty of clean fluids.
✔ Intravenous fluids in severe cases.
✔ Antibiotics prescribed by doctor.
✔ Hospital care if dehydration is severe.

Tablets Used for Cholera

Doctors may prescribe:

  • Doxycycline
  • Azithromycin
  • Ciprofloxacin
  • ORS (Oral Rehydration Salts)

⚠ Immediate treatment is important to prevent severe dehydration. Always follow medical advice.

Seek emergency medical help if there is severe dehydration, very low urine output, or unconsciousness.

Prevention Tips

  • Drink boiled or purified water
  • Wash hands regularly
  • Maintain proper sanitation
  • Eat properly cooked food
  • Get vaccinated if recommended
