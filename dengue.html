Complete Information About Dengue

Dengue

Dengue is a viral infection caused by the dengue virus and spread by infected Aedes mosquitoes. It is common in tropical and subtropical regions. Dengue can range from mild fever to severe life-threatening conditions.

Symptoms of Dengue

  • High fever (104°F / 40°C)
  • Severe headache
  • Pain behind the eyes
  • Muscle and joint pain
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Skin rash
  • Low platelet count

How Does Dengue Spread?

  • Through the bite of infected Aedes mosquito
  • Usually spreads during rainy season
  • Not spread directly from person to person

How to Cure / Treat Dengue?

✔ There is no specific antiviral cure for dengue.
✔ Drink plenty of fluids to prevent dehydration.
✔ Take adequate rest.
✔ Monitor platelet count regularly.
✔ Hospitalization may be required in severe cases.

Tablets Used in Dengue Treatment

Doctors usually recommend:

  • Paracetamol (for fever and pain)

⚠ Avoid Aspirin and Ibuprofen as they can increase bleeding risk. Always consult a doctor before taking any medicine.

Prevention Tips

  • Use mosquito repellent
  • Wear full-sleeved clothes
  • Remove stagnant water around home
  • Use mosquito nets
  • Keep surroundings clean
