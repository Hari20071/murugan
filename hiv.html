Complete Information About HIV/AIDS

HIV/AIDS

HIV (Human Immunodeficiency Virus) attacks the immune system and weakens the body’s ability to fight infections. If untreated, it can progress to AIDS (Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome), the advanced stage of HIV infection.

Symptoms of HIV

  • Fever
  • Fatigue
  • Swollen lymph nodes
  • Sore throat
  • Skin rash
  • Weight loss
  • Frequent infections

How Does HIV Spread?

  • Unprotected sexual contact
  • Sharing contaminated needles
  • Blood transfusion with infected blood
  • From mother to child during pregnancy or breastfeeding

How to Cure / Treat HIV?

✔ There is no complete cure for HIV.
✔ Antiretroviral Therapy (ART) helps control the virus.
✔ Take medicines daily as prescribed.
✔ Regular medical check-ups and blood tests.
✔ Healthy lifestyle and proper nutrition.

Tablets Used for HIV Treatment

Doctors prescribe Antiretroviral (ARV) medicines such as:

  • Tenofovir
  • Lamivudine
  • Efavirenz
  • Dolutegravir

⚠ HIV medicines must be taken exactly as prescribed. Skipping doses can reduce treatment effectiveness.

Early testing and treatment can help people with HIV live long, healthy lives. Consult a healthcare professional for proper guidance.

Prevention Tips

  • Practice safe sex
  • Do not share needles
  • Get tested regularly
  • Ensure safe blood transfusion
  • Follow medical advice during pregnancy
