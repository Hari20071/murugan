Complete Information About Typhoid Fever

Typhoid Fever

Typhoid fever is a serious bacterial infection caused by Salmonella typhi. It spreads through contaminated food and water and mainly affects the digestive system.

Symptoms of Typhoid Fever

  • High and prolonged fever
  • Headache
  • Weakness and fatigue
  • Abdominal pain
  • Constipation or diarrhea
  • Loss of appetite
  • Skin rash (rose spots)

How Does It Spread?

  • Contaminated food or water
  • Poor sanitation and hygiene
  • Close contact with infected person

How to Cure / Treat Typhoid?

✔ Take prescribed antibiotics completely.
✔ Drink plenty of clean fluids.
✔ Take adequate rest.
✔ Eat light and nutritious food.
✔ Hospital care in severe cases.

Tablets Used for Typhoid Fever

Doctors may prescribe:

  • Azithromycin
  • Cefixime
  • Ciprofloxacin
  • Paracetamol (for fever)

⚠ Always complete the full course of antibiotics. Do not self-medicate without doctor consultation.

Seek medical help if fever persists for more than 3 days or if there is severe abdominal pain.

Prevention Tips

  • Drink boiled or purified water
  • Wash hands regularly
  • Avoid street food from unhygienic places
  • Get vaccinated if recommended
  • Maintain proper sanitation
