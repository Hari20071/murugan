Complete Information About Malaria

Malaria

Malaria is a serious infectious disease caused by Plasmodium parasites. It is transmitted to humans through the bite of infected female Anopheles mosquitoes. It is common in tropical and subtropical regions.

Common Symptoms

  • High fever
  • Chills and shivering
  • Headache
  • Sweating
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Muscle pain
  • Fatigue

Causes

  • Plasmodium falciparum
  • Plasmodium vivax
  • Plasmodium malariae
  • Plasmodium ovale

How Malaria Spreads

  • Bite of infected Anopheles mosquito
  • Blood transfusion (rare cases)
  • From mother to baby (rare cases)

How to Cure / Treat Malaria?

✔ Immediate medical diagnosis
✔ Complete full course of prescribed medicines
✔ Drink plenty of fluids
✔ Take proper rest
✔ Monitor temperature regularly

Tablets Used for Malaria

Common medicines prescribed by doctors include:

  • Chloroquine
  • Artemisinin-based Combination Therapy (ACT)
  • Primaquine
  • Mefloquine
  • Doxycycline

⚠ Always consult a doctor before taking malaria medication. Incomplete treatment can lead to complications and drug resistance.

Prevention Tips

  • Use mosquito nets while sleeping
  • Apply mosquito repellent
  • Wear full-sleeve clothing
  • Avoid stagnant water around home
  • Indoor spraying in malaria-prone areas
