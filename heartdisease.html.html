Complete Information About Heart Disease

Heart Disease

Heart disease refers to a group of conditions that affect the heart and blood vessels. It includes coronary artery disease, heart attack, heart failure, and arrhythmias. It is one of the leading causes of death worldwide.

Types of Heart Disease

  • Coronary Artery Disease (CAD)
  • Heart Attack (Myocardial Infarction)
  • Heart Failure
  • Arrhythmia (Irregular heartbeat)
  • Congenital Heart Disease

Common Symptoms

  • Chest pain or discomfort
  • Shortness of breath
  • Fatigue
  • Irregular heartbeat
  • Swelling in legs or feet
  • Dizziness or fainting

Causes

  • High blood pressure
  • High cholesterol
  • Smoking
  • Diabetes
  • Obesity
  • Unhealthy diet
  • Lack of physical activity

Risk Factors

  • Age above 45 (men) / 55 (women)
  • Family history of heart disease
  • Stress
  • Excess alcohol consumption

How to Treat / Manage Heart Disease?

✔ Adopt heart-healthy diet
✔ Exercise regularly
✔ Maintain healthy weight
✔ Control blood pressure and cholesterol
✔ Stop smoking
✔ Regular medical checkups

Tablets Used for Heart Disease

Common medicines prescribed by doctors include:

  • Aspirin (blood thinner)
  • Atorvastatin (cholesterol control)
  • Clopidogrel
  • Beta blockers (e.g., Metoprolol)
  • ACE inhibitors (e.g., Enalapril)
  • Nitroglycerin

⚠ Never take heart medicines without doctor supervision. Immediate medical help is required for chest pain lasting more than a few minutes.

Prevention Tips

  • Eat fruits and vegetables daily
  • Reduce salt and saturated fats
  • Exercise at least 30 minutes daily
  • Avoid smoking and tobacco
  • Manage stress effectively
